About Us

A government-approved partner for
your Hajj & travel journey

H.M. Tours & Travels is a government-approved Hajj and travel service organisation, providing Hajj & Umrah services, air ticketing, hotel booking, visa support, passport assistance and complete holiday & tour planning.

Operating alongside our associated brand SILKWAY AIR SERVICE, our team supports pilgrims and travellers at every step — from documentation to departure and beyond.

Recognition

Honoured at the Mina Pilgrims Services Hospitality Center

H.M. Tours & Travels was honoured with a certificate of recognition at the Mina Pilgrims Services Hospitality Center (Hospitality Center 3, serving Bangladeshi pilgrims) in Saudi Arabia, presented by the center's leadership in appreciation of the agency's service to Hajj pilgrims during the season. The award reflects years of coordinated, on-ground support for our pilgrims throughout their journey to the Holy Sites.
